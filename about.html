About Me

My Journey

From Kerala to JNU, from corporate strategy to Rice University, and now an incoming PhD candidate at Rutgers Business School — shaped by curiosity, resilience, and a desire to contribute meaningfully.

The Path So Far

My career has been shaped by a combination of rigorous academic training and hands-on corporate experience across strategy, finance, and economics. I've worked with global firms, driven measurable results, and spent years teaching and mentoring at the university level.

I drove 40% revenue growth at VAS Carbons, managed credit and risk on a $75M+ portfolio at Neste, and consulted for firms like Sasol and Toshiba. Along the way, I interviewed over 250 candidates across roles and industries — an experience that profoundly shaped how I think about hiring, preparation, and professional positioning.

Beyond the corporate world, I've taught economics at the university level and actively mentor students and aspirants who value conceptual clarity over shortcuts.

Resilience

Surviving Guillain-Barré Syndrome fundamentally reshaped how I approach learning, work, and leadership. It taught me to value depth over speed, and to bring patience and perspective to everything I do.

Current Focus

I am an incoming PhD candidate in International Business at Rutgers Business School, where I will be pursuing research at the intersection of global strategy, institutions, and economic decision-making. Alongside this, I continue to offer selective professional services in consulting, tutoring, and coaching. I work only with individuals and organisations who value clarity, rigour, and honest guidance.
