The question I keep returning to

How do firms and organizations adapt when the rules of the game are unclear or shifting?

Full research statement →

  1. Institutional strategy

    How firms read and respond to changing rules, norms, and regulation across borders and markets.

  2. Behavioral strategy

    How cognition, judgment, and incentives shape decisions under genuine uncertainty.

  3. Quantitative methods

    Causal inference, econometrics, and simulation, rigorous enough to make the claims credible.

Grounded in practice

Theory tested against real decisions.
