What this site collects

This is a personal website. It collects as little as it can.

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics sets cookies and records an approximate location, device type and the pages you view. Visitors in Europe are asked before these cookies are set and can decline.

Forms

If you subscribe to new articles, your email address is sent to me through Formspree and used only to email you when a new article is published. If you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to me the same way and used only to reply.

I do not sell or share this information.

Your choices
