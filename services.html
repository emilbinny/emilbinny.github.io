Services

How I Can Help

A focused set of services designed for professionals, students, and aspirants who value depth, clarity, and results.

01

Interview & Resume Coaching

What This Is

Practical, structured interview preparation grounded in real hiring experience — not guesswork or templates. Having interviewed over 250 candidates across roles and industries, I bring the perspective of the hiring side to your preparation.

What You Get

  • Mock interviews with detailed, honest feedback
  • Resume and positioning review — clarity over buzzwords
  • Structured frameworks for behavioural and case-based questions
  • Industry-specific preparation for consulting, finance, and corporate roles

Who This Is For

Candidates preparing for competitive interviews who want to go beyond surface-level advice. Particularly suited for MBA admissions, consulting, finance, and senior corporate roles.

02

Economic Research & Writing

What This Is

Rigorous research and analytical writing on economics, public policy, and related domains. Work that is thorough, well-sourced, and built to withstand scrutiny — done properly, not rushed.

What You Get

  • Research papers and policy briefs
  • Data-driven economic analysis
  • Literature reviews and synthesis
  • Clear, structured writing on complex topics

Who This Is For

Academics, policy professionals, students, and organisations who need high-quality economic research and writing — whether for publication, decision-making, or strategic planning.

03

Economics & MBA Tutoring

What This Is

Concept-first teaching for students who want to understand, not just pass. I focus on building real understanding of economic principles and business frameworks — the kind that stays with you beyond the exam.

What You Get

  • One-on-one or small-group tutoring sessions
  • Coverage of micro and macroeconomics, econometrics, and finance
  • MBA-level strategy, accounting, and quantitative methods
  • Exam preparation with emphasis on conceptual depth

Who This Is For

Undergraduate and graduate students in economics, MBA candidates, and UPSC/competitive exam aspirants who value deep understanding over rote preparation.

04

Business & Strategy Consulting

What This Is

Structured support on pricing, finance, market entry, and decision-making — grounded in real corporate experience with firms like Sasol, Toshiba, and Neste. Clear thinking applied to real business problems.

What You Get

  • Market analysis and competitive positioning
  • Financial modelling and pricing strategy
  • Business plan review and strategic advisory
  • Decision frameworks for complex, high-stakes situations

Who This Is For

Small businesses, startups, and professionals facing strategic decisions who need structured, experienced thinking — not generic advice.
