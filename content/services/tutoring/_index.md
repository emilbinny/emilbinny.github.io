## Economics & MBA Tutoring

Passing an economics exam is one thing. Actually understanding how economies work — and being able to apply that thinking in the real world — is something else entirely.

Most tutoring focuses on the exam. I focus on the concept first, then connect it to the exam. The result is that you don't just score well — you retain it, and you can use it when it actually matters.

---

### Who This Is For

- MBA students struggling with economics or finance coursework
- Undergraduate students preparing for economics-heavy exams or placements
- Working professionals revisiting economics for a career shift or further study
- Anyone who has been taught economics before but never truly understood it

---

### What We Cover

**Core Economics** — micro, macro, and applied economics. Built from first principles, not memorisation.

**MBA-Level Subjects** — managerial economics, corporate finance, strategy fundamentals. Linked to real business decisions so the material actually sticks.

**Exam Preparation** — once the concepts are solid, we work on exam technique — how to structure answers, handle tricky questions, and score well under pressure.

---

### How It Works

The first session is diagnostic. We figure out where the gaps actually are — not where you think they are. From there, every session is targeted. No generic explanations, no wasted time on things you already understand.

Most students see a noticeable shift in clarity within three to four sessions. Some need more, depending on scope. We'll be honest about that from the start.
