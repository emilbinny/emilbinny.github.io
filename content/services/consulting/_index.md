## Business & Strategy Consulting

Most advice you'll get is generic. "Analyse the market." "Understand your competitors." "Build a strategy." None of that is wrong — but none of it tells you what to actually do next.

This is different. I work with founders and professionals who have a real problem — pricing, market entry, financial planning, or a decision that's stuck — and I help them think through it clearly and move forward.

My experience spans corporate strategy at firms like Sasol and Toshiba, credit and risk management with portfolios over $75M, and driving 40% revenue growth at VAS Carbons. That's the foundation this work is built on.

---

### Who This Is For

- Founders who need structured thinking on a key business decision but don't have an in-house strategy team
- Professionals navigating a complex career or business pivot and want an outside perspective
- Small teams that need a pricing, market entry, or financial plan — done properly, not guessed at

### What We Work On

**Decision Frameworks** — when you're stuck on a choice, the problem is usually that you don't have a clear way to evaluate the options. We build that structure together.

**Pricing & Revenue Strategy** — not just "what should I charge" but why, how it positions you, and how to adjust as you grow.

**Market Entry & Competitive Analysis** — before you move into a new market or launch something new, you need to know what you're walking into. That's what this covers.

**Financial Planning** — clear, honest financial projections that account for reality — not just the best-case scenario.
